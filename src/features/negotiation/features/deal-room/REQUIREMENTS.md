# Deal room — business requirements

Folder: `src/features/negotiation/features/deal-room/`  ·  Top level: [REQUIREMENTS.md](../../../../../REQUIREMENTS.md)
Parent: [Negotiation](../../REQUIREMENTS.md)

## Purpose

Show a deal to its owner clearly.

## Who it serves

Parent owner.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEG-ROM-01 | Show the transcript in the owner language alongside the original. | Must | Both texts visible. |
| NEG-ROM-02 | Show deal state as a simple timeline. | Must | Current state is obvious at a glance. |
| NEG-ROM-03 | Show owner questions inline as cards. | Must | A pending question is the most prominent element. |
| NEG-ROM-04 | Work on a phone-width screen. | Must | No horizontal scrolling at 375 px. |

## Out of scope

- Typing directly to the other owner.

## Traceability

BR-05, BR-06, BR-15.
