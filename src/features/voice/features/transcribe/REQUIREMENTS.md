## Purpose

Turn speech into text.

## Who it serves

Voice.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| VOI-TRN-01 | Transcribe voice notes in supported languages. | Must | Meets accuracy on the sample set. |
| VOI-TRN-02 | Fall back to typing when transcription fails. | Must | Typing is always available. |

## Out of scope

- Speaker identification.
