# Category rules — business requirements

## Purpose

The rules per category.

## Who it serves

Safety.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAF-RUL-01 | Car seats require a crash-history declaration. | Must | Listing without it is blocked. |
| SAF-RUL-02 | Cribs, strollers and high chairs show a recall-check prompt. | Must | Prompt appears on the item. |
| SAF-RUL-03 | Block categories that cannot be safely reused (for example medicines and consumables). | Must | Blocklist is enforced at listing. |

## Out of scope

- Safety certification.
